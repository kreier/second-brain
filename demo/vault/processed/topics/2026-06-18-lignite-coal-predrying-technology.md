Lignite is a low-rank coal characterized by high intrinsic moisture content, which severely lowers boiler combustion efficiency and increases parasitic energy loss spent evaporating water during burning.

### Pre-Drying Approaches
- **Exhaust Gas Fluidized Bed Drying**: Uses flue gas waste heat to dry coal particles in a fluidized bed prior to boiler injection.
- **Hot Air Drying**: Directs heated air streams across raw lignite feed.
- **Mechanical Thermal Dewatering**: Applies heat and pressure to squeeze out moisture mechanically before combustion.

Pre-drying increases net calorific value and reduces parasitic boiler load.

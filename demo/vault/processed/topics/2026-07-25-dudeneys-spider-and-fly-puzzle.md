Henry Dudeney's classic "spider and fly" puzzle asks for the shortest path (geodesic) a spider can crawl along the interior surfaces of a rectangular cuboid room to reach a fly positioned on the opposite wall.

### Key Insight
- **Counter-Intuitive Shortest Path**: The shortest surface path is rarely the obvious direct route (e.g., across the floor and up the wall). Depending on room dimensions, the shortest path crosses five surfaces, traveling across side walls and ceiling/floor.
- **Solution Method**: Solved by unfolding the 3D room into flat 2D net configurations and connecting the spider and fly positions with straight line segments across unfolded faces.

The Hongsa power plant in Xayaburi province, Laos (near the Thai border) is a major thermal power facility in a national energy sector otherwise dominated by hydropower.

### Key Characteristics
- **Fuel Source**: Mine-mouth power plant burning locally mined lignite coal (low-rank coal), eliminating fuel import costs.
- **BOT Structure**: Developed as a Build-Operate-Transfer project where a private consortium operates the plant for a fixed concession period before transferring ownership to the Lao government.
- **Power Offtake**: Primary electricity generation is exported to Thailand via Electricity Generating Authority of Thailand (EGAT) long-term power purchase agreements (PPAs).

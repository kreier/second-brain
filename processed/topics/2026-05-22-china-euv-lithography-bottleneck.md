The primary technological bottleneck for domestic leading-edge semiconductor fabrication in China is extreme ultraviolet (EUV) lithography hardware, rather than chip process engineering knowledge.

### Bottleneck Components
- **Light Source & Precision Optics**: Manufacturing commercial-grade high-power EUV light sources and defect-free reflective optics remains the critical technological hurdle.
- **Supply Chain Concentration**: ASML holds a commercial monopoly on production EUV scanner systems. Domestic Chinese initiatives (including SMEE) remain behind ASML's commercial deployment benchmarks.
